### `NEEDS-DEVICE-DATA` Items
1. **Multi-vendor STP `root_bridge` outputs** (`Cisco NX-OS`, `Cisco IOS-XR`, `Arista EOS`, `Aruba AOS-CX`, `HP ProCurve`): needed to verify every priority+MAC bridge-ID syntax normalized by `compute_stp_root_consensus()` and `_compute_stp_root_scopes()` (TP-15).
2. **Active/Standby HA & FHRP pair captures** (`Cisco HSRP/VRRP/GLBP`, `OPNsense CARP`, `FortiGate FGCP`, `Palo Alto HA`): needed to confirm exact `l3_interfaces`, per-interface `mac_address`, and `fhrp` payload shapes on both peers for FHRP MAC/VIP dedupe and `duplicate_gateway_ip` exemption (TP-04, TP-12).
3. **Mixed-vendor abbreviated LLDP/CDP transcripts** (`Cisco IOS-XE/NX-OS/XR` + `Arista EOS` + `Juniper` using `Twe`, `Tw`, `TenGigE`, `Bundle-Ether`/`BE`, `Port-channel`/`Po`, `Et1`/`Eth1`/`Ethernet1`): needed to validate canonical interface matching end-to-end across drivers and topology modules (TP-01, TP-02, TP-03).
4. **Non-1:1 Junos `irb.<unit>` -> `vlan-id` and FortiGate `set vlanid` named VLAN interface captures**: needed to verify SVI-to-VLAN join edge cases beyond OPNsense `vtnet0_vlan*` and dot1Q subinterfaces (TP-11).
5. **Non-MAC LLDP Chassis-ID subtype captures** (subtypes 1–7, especially Local serial-number and IPv6 Network Address chassis IDs on `Cisco`/`Juniper`/`Aruba`/`ProCurve`): needed to confirm `resolve_neighbor_ip()` coverage beyond Norris IPv4/Local/MAC-port examples (TP-07).
