# Phase 1 Findings: `drivers-oss` (Role 3c — MikroTik, OPNsense, OpenWrt, FRR)

Source: Swarm Board `#18` (`drivers-oss`, 2026-11-24 20:17:33)

Phase 1 OSS & SMB Router Drivers audit complete (`mikrotik.py`, `opnsense.py`, `openwrt.py`, `frr.py`; strictly read-only, zero edits).

### 1. Read-Only & Paging Validation Summary
- **Zero Blast Radius**: All commands issued across all 4 drivers are **strictly read-only** (0 write/config/reboot/clear commands).
- **Paging & Transport**:
  - `MikroTikDriver`: Uses Netmiko `mikrotik_routeros` (`MikrotikBase._modify_connection_params()` appends `+ct511w4098h`; `disable_paging()` is a no-op returning `""`). **Gap (`DO-05`)**: `4098h` is a finite height, not applied on Telnet, and `mikrotik.py` omits `without-paging` on all table `print` commands despite `docs/drivers/mikrotik.md:30` & `overview.md:47`.
  - `OPNsenseDriver`: Non-interactive Paramiko `exec_command` (no PTY/pager).
  - `OpenWrtDriver` / `FRRDriver`: Netmiko `linux` (`LinuxSSH.disable_paging()` is a no-op; `vtysh -c '...'` is single-command non-paged).
- **Logging Audit**: No bare `except: pass` blocks, **but** parser/JSON/schema exceptions are logged only at `logger.debug` across all of `mikrotik.py`, `openwrt.py`, `frr.py`, and `opnsense.py:320-323,422-423` (`DO-25`), hiding silent empty-list returns (e.g. `test_frr_driver_plain_linux_server_fallback` swallows an `AssertionError` on `ip -s -j link show`!).
