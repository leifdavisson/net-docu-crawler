### 2. Explicit `"data not collected / parse failed"` Status Contract (Schema v2.1)
Backwards-compatible additions across `schema.py`, `base.py`, `quality_gates.py`, and `report_generator.py`:
- **`COLLECTION_STATES`** (`schema.py`): `"ok"`, `"not_applicable"`, `"not_collected"`, `"command_failed"`, `"parse_failed"`, `"partial"`.
- **`COLLECTION_SECTIONS`** (`schema.py`): `version`, `interfaces`, `neighbors`, `routes`, `arp`, `mac_table`, `stp`, `poe`, `optics`, `running_config`, `vlans`, `port_profiles`, `vrfs`, `routing_peers`, `fhrp`, `dhcp`, `services`, `security_policy`, `l2_operational`, `discovery_protocols`.
- **Per-device fields** (`device_data` in `schema.py` + `base.py`):
  - `collection_status: dict[str, str]` — populated by `BaseDriver.crawl()`; unoverridden `BaseDriver` hooks record `"not_applicable"` (when persona doesn't apply, e.g. STP on non-switch) or `"not_collected"` (driver lacks command); CLI error/exception sets `"command_failed"`; parser exception/empty-on-nonempty-output sets `"parse_failed"`; if `running_config != "ok"`, config-derived sections inherit `"not_collected"`/`"command_failed"`. Legacy datasets missing keys default to `"not_collected"`, **never** `"ok"`.
  - `parse_errors: list[dict]` — `{section, command, stage ("command"|"parse"), error, raw_snippet}` (`raw_snippet` redacted & `<=200` chars, zero credentials).
  - Centralize `CLI_ERROR_MARKERS` in `schema.py` (shared by `base.py` & `quality_gates.py`).
- **Report rendering helper** (`report_generator.py`): `_section_availability_summary(devices, section)` returns counts by state. **Hard invariant**: NO section may render `✓`, `"None found"`, `"Disabled"`, `"Unconfigured"`, or `"Healthy"` when any applicable device has `command_failed` / `parse_failed` / `not_collected`; instead emit explicit `⚠️ Data not collected (N/M devices)` or `❌ Collection/parse failed (N/M devices)` with per-host attribution.
- **Quality gate** (`quality_gates.py`): Add `GATE_COLLECTION_INTEGRITY` (P1 warning / P0 if `interfaces` or `running_config` failed on crawled infra) + `collection_coverage` breakdown in `collection_manifest.json` and `data_quality_report.md`.

### 3. CSV & Mermaid Audit Summary
- **CSV Audit**: **0 manual string-built CSVs** — all 14 CSV outputs in `report_generator.py` use `csv.DictWriter(..., newline="", encoding="utf-8")` (existing CSVs in `testbed_rebuilt`/`norris_rebuilt` have 0 column-count mismatches). Confirmed bugs: (1) `interfaces.csv` (`L5191`) & `svi_routes.csv` (`L5261`) raise `TypeError` on `",".join()` when lists contain `int`s (e.g. `allowed_vlans=[10,20]`); (2) all 14 CSV writers pass raw `\r`/`\n` (breaking line-oriented parsers) and untrusted spreadsheet formula prefixes (`=`, `+`, `@`, non-numeric `-`, `\t`); (3) `generate_cabling_matrix` (`L2453`) & `export_layered_csvs` (`L5488`) lack `_is_network_infra()` filter, leaking `pve`/`librenms` into CSVs; (4) zero Markdown table cell escaping (`|`, `\n`) in `report_generator.py` & `quality_gates.py`.
- **Mermaid Audit**: `_mermaid_safe_text()` (`report_generator.py:412-420`) only escapes `"`, `\r`, `\n`, `<`, `>`. It leaves `|`, `#`, `;`, `` ` ``, `%%`, `[`, `]`, `{`, `}`, `(`, `)`, and trailing `\` unescaped — offline repro proves `"Uplink\\"` produces `sw_0["Uplink\"]` and fails `validate_mermaid_block`. Structural badges (`[Metro-E]`, `[STP BLK]`, `<br/>`) must be assembled *after* escaping raw device fields. Also confirmed: `.strip("<br/>")` at `L1559` strips trailing `b`/`r` chars (`"Lab"`->`"La"`, `"Server"`->`"Serve"`); `_mermaid_slug()` (`L422`) can emit reserved Mermaid keywords (`end`, `subgraph`, `class`, `click`, `style`, `default`); `L1336` strips leading space before `dup_ip`; `_compact_mermaid_block_if_oversized` (`L580-611`) misses stadium `ext_*([` nodes; `validate_mermaid_block` failures at `L1141,1808` are only `print()`ed; `diagram_validator.py:27-45` misses unescaped `|` in `|"..."|` labels, mid-line `%%`, unbalanced `subgraph`/`end` & brackets, reserved node IDs, out-of-bounds `linkStyle`, and `>50000` char `maxTextSize`.
